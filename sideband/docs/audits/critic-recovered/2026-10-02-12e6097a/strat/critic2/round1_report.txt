**2. BLOCKING — the COVERAGE-EFFECT mechanism claim is wrong on the largest fallback stratum (BLUEPRINT 7040-7042, 7044, 6876-6877; tool 436-439).**
- The text says: "where the free tables read the marginal and any informative block beats them". By construction:
  - At cur-unseen positions, TABLE_CUR reads the marginal in both arms, and the free arm's TABLE_PREV is informative.
  - At prev-unseen positions, free_bigram collapses to free_tok.
  - Only at both-unseen positions (0.0113 of test positions) do the free tables read nothing but the marginal.
